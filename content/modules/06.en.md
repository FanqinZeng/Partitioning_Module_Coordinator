## 01 · Use global structure to order vertices

Spectral bisection relaxes discrete labels into continuous coordinates, encourages strongly connected vertices to stay close, then converts coordinates back into a split. Here L=D−A is the unnormalized Laplacian. This is neither a normalized-cut implementation nor a physical spring simulation.

For coordinates x, xᵀLx=Σedges w(u,v)(xu−xv)². A heavy edge with widely separated endpoints costs more energy. Constant coordinates have zero energy but no grouping information, so exclude the constant direction and fix the vector length.

## 02 · Build L edge by edge

For u–v of weight w, add w to Luu and Lvv and subtract w from Luv and Lvu. In G01, A has weighted degree 6, giving first row [6,−5,−1,0,0] in A–E order. The matrix is symmetric, has nonnegative diagonal and zero row sums.

```text
for each edge (u,v,w):
    L[u,u] += w; L[v,v] += w
    L[u,v] -= w; L[v,u] -= w
```

Counting both adjacency-list copies doubles the matrix. Some orders may survive, but eigenvalues and energies change. Verify the model before judging the final partition.

## 03 · The vector and its diagnostics

Sort eigenvalues increasingly. A connected graph has smallest eigenvalue 0 with a constant eigenvector. An eigenvector for the second smallest eigenvalue λ2 is a Fiedler vector. The page solves the symmetric problem live using Jacobi rotations and reports residual ‖Lx−λ2x‖/‖x‖, orthogonality to constants and the λ2–λ3 gap.

Both x and −x are valid. A sign flip reverses the ordering and exchanges side names. To stabilize display, the first significant entry in name order is made positive. Equal entries need deterministic ties; numerical errors should not be hidden behind a hand-authored ordering.

## 04 · Sweep every legal prefix

Sort vertices by ascending coordinate and try prefixes of lengths 1 through n−1. Check the count window and compute each cut. By default each side needs at least 20% of the vertices. This is a count rule, not an area bound.

G01 orders E,D,C,B,A. Prefix cuts are 6,3,5,6, making length 2 best: DE | ABC. Cutting at the middle or at zero alone can miss the best legal threshold.

## 05 · Disconnection and repeated eigenvalues

Multiple connected components produce multiple zero eigenvalues and a non-unique second vector. A connected graph can also have λ2=λ3; the equal-weight four-cycle has both equal to 2. Different valid solvers may return different directions in the same eigenspace.

The lab displays these warnings. Check residual, feasibility, energy and objective rather than demanding identical vector entries or screenshots. If no prefix satisfies the window, report infeasible instead of substituting an illegal midpoint.

## 06 · A relaxation is not an exact solver

Continuous coordinates provide structural guidance. Prefix cuts cover only a small fraction of discrete assignments, and rounding must still satisfy balance. G01's optimum of 3 does not establish a general guarantee.

A tiny exhaustive search can expose a gap. A worse spectral cut does not by itself imply a numerical bug. First verify the matrix, residual and constraints; then consider the loss introduced by relaxation and rounding.
