01 / AN ORIGINAL INTRODUCTION

What is partitioning?

Start with a two-team assignment and see why a small cut alone is not enough. A shared five-vertex graph introduces cut cost, counts and areas, followed by construction, KL/FM, spectral and k-way methods. Learn where to begin and what to inspect in the labs.

一个项目，需要两个小组协作。A project needs two collaborating teams.

联系紧密的任务，最好留在一起。Keep closely linked tasks together.

全放一组，跨组沟通可以为零。One part can have zero crossings.

但空间与工作量，不允许这样分。Space and workload can forbid that assignment.

Partitioning：在约束下划分。Partitioning means dividing under constraints.

芯片单元彼此连线，也需要分区。Connected circuit cells also need regions.

跨区域连接，是这里衡量的代价。Cross-region connections contribute to our cost.

先用五个节点，把代价算清楚。Use five vertices to make the cost explicit.

边权表示切开这条连接的代价。An edge weight is the cost of cutting that connection.

跨组边的权重，逐条相加。Add the crossing edge weights, one by one.

总和为12，每条无向边只算一次。The sum is 12. Count each undirected edge once.

换成 ABC | DE，代价会怎样？Try ABC | DE. What happens to the cut?

cut 为 3，人数仍是二与三。The cut is 3; the counts are still two and three.

人数平衡，不等于面积可行。Balanced counts do not guarantee feasible areas.

A 面积变为 4，ABC 面积变为 6。Set A's area to 4: ABC then has area 6.

五个节点，有十五种非空二分。Five vertices have 15 nonempty, unlabeled splits.

若限定二与三，则剩十种。Requiring counts two and three leaves ten.

一百个节点，候选已是三十位数。At 100 vertices, the count already has 30 digits.

逐一试遍，很快就不再现实。Trying every split soon becomes impractical.

先构造起点：随机、贪心、生长。Construct a start: random, greedy, or growing.

生长吸收 B，得到 cut 5。Growing adds B, producing cut 5.

KL 成对交换，FM 单点移动。KL exchanges a pair; FM moves one vertex.

先试探、锁定，再选最佳前缀。Try, lock, then keep the best prefix.

谱方法用矩阵结构，给节点排序。Spectral structure orders the vertices.

扫描合法切口，选择较小的 cut。Sweep legal thresholds for a smaller cut.

继续二分，就能得到更多分组。Repeated bisection produces more parts.

每次在当前子图上重新计算。Each split solves its current induced subgraph.

同一张图，比较不同多路方法。Compare k-way methods on the same graph.

先检查容量，再比较 cut。Check capacity before comparing cut.

从割代价开始，用实验和代码检验判断。Start with cut cost. Test your reasoning with labs and code.

Multiway partitioning
