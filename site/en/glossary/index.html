LEARNING PARTITIONING / REFERENCE

Glossary

Bilingual terms with links back to their lessons. Press / to search the complete lesson text.

节点 / vertex
A circuit cell with a positive size. Lesson 02 ↗
边权 / weight
The cost of a crossing connection; this lab uses positive weights. Lesson 02 ↗
割代价 / cut
The sum of crossing undirected edge weights, each counted once. Lesson 02 ↗
容量 / capacity
The maximum total area allowed in one part. Lesson 02 ↗
可行 / feasible
A complete assignment with nonempty parts and the stated capacity. Lesson 02 ↗
构造 / construction
Building an assignment from scratch. Lesson 03 ↗
种子 / seed
An integer controlling the pseudorandom sequence. Lesson 03 ↗
增量 / increment
The new crossing cost to already assigned nodes. Lesson 03 ↗
区域生长 / region growing
Expand an anchored part using connection strength. Lesson 03 ↗
平票 / tie
Equal scores resolved by a declared deterministic rule. Lesson 03 ↗
D 值 / D value
External minus internal edge weight. Lesson 04 ↗
增益 / gain
cut_before − cut_after; positive means improvement. Lesson 04 ↗
锁定 / lock
A tried node cannot be selected again in this pass. Lesson 04 ↗
pass
A sequence of disjoint trial choices from a pass start. Lesson 04 ↗
最佳前缀 / best prefix
The earliest prefix of maximum cumulative gain, including the empty prefix. Lesson 04 ↗
合法候选 / legal move
Both sides remain nonempty and count difference is at most maxDiff. Lesson 05 ↗
FS
Number of pins of a net on the moving node's from side. Lesson 05 ↗
TE
Number of pins of a net on the target side. Lesson 05 ↗
gain bucket
Candidates indexed by gain; this implementation uses a Map and scans legal nodes. Lesson 05 ↗
回退 / rollback
Undo every trial after the best prefix. Lesson 05 ↗
拉普拉斯 / Laplacian
L=D−A, with weighted degrees on D's diagonal. Lesson 06 ↗
Fiedler 向量
An eigenvector for the second smallest eigenvalue. Lesson 06 ↗
残差 / residual
‖Lx−λx‖/‖x‖, a check of numerical solution quality. Lesson 06 ↗
扫描 / sweep
Sort by eigenvector value and test each prefix. Lesson 06 ↗
简并 / degeneracy
Repeated eigenvalues make the vector direction non-unique. Lesson 06 ↗
诱导子图 / induced subgraph
The chosen vertices and all edges with both endpoints in that set. Lesson 07 ↗
叶子 / leaf
A current part that can still be split. Lesson 07 ↗
分裂树 / split tree
A record of each parent part and its two children. Lesson 07 ↗
新增割 / added cut
The increase in full-graph cut caused by a split. Lesson 07 ↗
局部约束 / local constraint
A rule for the current split, not a guarantee on every final part. Lesson 07 ↗
k 路 / k-way
Assign vertices to k disjoint, nonempty parts. Lesson 08 ↗
轮转 / round robin
Assign vertex i to i mod k. Lesson 08 ↗
交换改进 / swap refinement
Accept a strictly improving, capacity-legal cross-part swap. Lesson 08 ↗
局部最优 / local optimum
No improvement in the chosen neighborhood; not a global guarantee. Lesson 08 ↗
精确枚举 / exact oracle
Enumerate legal tiny assignments for calibration and tests. Lesson 08 ↗
